ALPINE · IPHONE PROTOTYPE

Music for the mountain.

Alpine is a personal skiing music companion in development. Choose a soundtrack for the lift and the descent, with music playback through Apple Music.

Song-data attribution

Optional playlist scanning uses BPM, acousticness and danceability data supplied by GetSongBPM. These characteristics support editable suggestions, not guaranteed measurements of a song's energy. No audio is uploaded for scanning.

Prototype privacy

Apple manages music authorization. With your permission, playlist scans send song titles and artists to GetSongBPM. Album names are used locally for matching. Alpine does not send Apple credentials, audio or location to GetSongBPM.

The current prototype stores preferences and tags locally, keeps lookup results in memory, and stores a personally entered API key in the device's Keychain. It has no analytics or app server. The website's hosting provider handles website traffic.

This page describes the prototype, which is not yet an App Store release. Music-service access and playback depend on each service's requirements.
